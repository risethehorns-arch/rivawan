III

Resonance الرنين

Sound is present in all of Rawan's facilitation. In her SoundWork sessions it becomes the main language — an invitation to engage your own voice, to open blockages, bring balance, transform emotion and discover new parts of yourself inside the collective frequency of harmony.

— listening for a tone

Try it · The Cymatics Lab

Sound has a shape.

Every tone draws its own figure in the sand. Choose a maqam and play its notes, let it walk up and down by itself, or hum into your microphone — and watch the grains find the still lines.

Tuned to A = 432 Hz, like the garden you hear when sound is on. Your voice is analysed on this device only — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

In circle

Group sound-healing journeys, often alongside other sound healers and musicians, where every voice in the room becomes part of the instrument.

    Her Story الحكاية

    1. Kuwait

      Born to Palestinian diaspora parents.

    2. Amman

      At three, her family took refuge in Jordan during the Gulf War. Nine years of childhood here.

    3. London, Ontario

      At twelve, to Canada — middle school and high school.

    4. Toronto

      A BA at the University of Toronto, double major in Political Science and Near & Middle Eastern Civilizations.

    5. Indonesia

      The first journey out into the world — and the bubble bursts.

    6. Palestine

      Her ancestral homeland, visited for the first time.

    7. Jordan

      A year as Communications Coordinator with Madrasati — and the knowledge that she could not be kept behind a desk.

    8. Toronto, again

      Facilitation training with the Institute of Cultural Affairs.

    9. India

      Exploring the outer and inner realms in parallel.

    10. Everywhere since

      Based in Jordan, flying to Nepal, Egypt, Spain, Turkey, Morocco, Syria, Lebanon and beyond — photography, music, facilitation, festival organising, community catalysing, movement and sound healing. Co-founder of SemaZen, and most recently of the Qamarjan World Music Project.
